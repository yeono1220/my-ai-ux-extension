A day with the guide
Meet Sunja, 72.

She lives alone and finds today's websites overwhelming — tiny menus, endless tabs, and words she doesn't recognize. Here's how one ordinary morning goes with the guide by her side.

Question 1
🏛️ National Tax Service
Sunja recently sold her home, and one worry keeps nagging at her: does she owe capital gains tax? She opens the guide on the National Tax Service site to find out.
"Am I subject to capital gains tax?"
Step by step, the guide leads her to the right page — where she learns the sale can be tax-exempt, but only if her home meets certain conditions.
➜ but does she qualify?
Question 2
📄 Internet Registry Office
To check whether she meets those exemption conditions, Sunja needs her official property records. So she opens the same guide again — this time on the Internet Registry Office.
"I need to issue my property register."
From search → select property → issue, the guide walks her through getting her property register (등기부등본) — the very document her exemption depends on.
One guide. Every website.
From a tax question to the document that answers it — Sunja followed one thread, and never got lost.
