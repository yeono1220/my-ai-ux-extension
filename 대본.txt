[화면 1] landing.html

 1-A) 맨 위 타이틀
   "Hi everyone. Have you ever opened a website and had no idea
    where to click? For people who didn't grow up online, that
    feeling happens every single day. We're Team Ieum, and we
    built a step guide that walks them through it."

 1-B) 라이브 프리뷰
   "Here's the idea. You just say where you want to go - by typing,
    or by voice - and the AI reads the page, finds the path, and
    lights up exactly which button to press, one step at a time,
    until you arrive."

 1-C) How it works (4단계)
   "Behind the scenes it's four steps: it reads the live page,
    the AI maps out the clicks, and it guides you through them -
    so it works on almost any website, even ones that change as
    you click."

 1-D) Meet Sunja (스토리)
   "Let me show you with a real situation. This is Sunja,
    seventy-two. She just sold the apartment she lived in for
    years, and now she's worried about one thing: will she have
    to pay a big capital gains tax on that sale? She doesn't even
    know who to ask. Watch how the guide helps her figure it out
    - across two different government websites."


[화면 2] demo_store.html

   "But first - how does Sunja get it? It's simple. On the Chrome
    Web Store, she just clicks 'Add to Chrome.'"


[화면 3] install.html

   "She confirms, pins it to the toolbar, and enters the key once.
    About thirty seconds later, the Step Guide icon is sitting right
    there, ready on any website she visits."


[화면 4] 국세청 (실제 사이트) — 시연 ①

   "Her first stop is the National Tax Service - the office that
    would actually know. But the site is buried in menus and tax
    words she's never seen. So she just clicks the Step Guide icon and
    asks, in plain language -"

       🎤  "양도소득세 대상인가요?"

   "The guide lights up each button and walks her straight to the
    answer. And the answer is good news: because this was her only
    home, the sale CAN be exempt from capital gains tax - but only
    if she owned it long enough. So now there's one thing she has
    to prove: how long she's actually owned the place."


[화면 5] 인터넷 등기소 (실제 사이트) — 시연 ②

   "That proof - the date she bought the home - lives on a totally
    different website: the Internet Registry Office. The document
    she needs is her property register, and she's never used this
    site in her life. So she opens the same guide again, and says -"

       🎤  "등기부등본을 떼고 싶어요"

   "And just like before, step by step, it walks her through
    searching her address, choosing her home, and issuing the
    document - the one piece of paper that proves she qualifies
    for the tax exemption."


[화면 6] landing.html (맨 위) — 맺음말

   "One question led to another, across two sites Sunja had never
    understood - and the same guide carried her through both,
    without ever getting lost. That's Step Guide: making the complex web
    easy for everyone. Thank you."
